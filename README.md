# lockin.gg

A voice coach for League of Legends that runs on your own PC. It talks to you during the game: when
dragon and Baron are coming, when enemies are dead long enough to take something, when you hit a power
spike, and what to do after you die. Hold a key to ask it anything. A small box above the minimap shows
your next item. After the game it writes a short debrief.

This repository only holds the installers and updates.

## Install

1. Download **LockinApp-win-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).
2. Run it. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. lockin.gg opens in your browser on its **Set up lockin.gg** page. Follow the three steps.
4. In League, set **Settings → Video → Window Mode** to **Borderless** for the item overlay.

Installed copies update themselves.

Needs Windows 10 or 11, a graphics card with 6 GB of video memory or more, a microphone and a headset.
